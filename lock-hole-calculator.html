Lock holes & lock slots
Lock holes (cut in the door face)

Example drawing, it follows the numbers on the right. You can drag a hole in any direction with your finger or mouse: up/down changes the gap, left/right changes “From door edge”. Up = toward the door TOP.

Every lock has different holes: round, long, short, wide, thin. Add them yourself. “Gap to handle centre” is the distance from the handle centre to the near end of the hole. To move a hole to the other side of the handle, press “Flip side” or just drag it in the drawing.

Handle centre → lock-body centre
Know your lock

Sample photo. The lines only show what to measure and are not to scale for your lock. The final version will use your own photo.

  • Faceplate

    The thin steel plate set into the door edge. Length × width × thickness 241 × 24 × 3. It needs a “panel slot” in the door edge.

  • Lock case

    The metal box hidden inside the door. Height × width × depth 188 × 21 × 100. It needs a “case hole” in the door edge.

  • Handle centre

    Where the handle spindle goes through, which is the centre of the round hole in the door face.

  • Case centre

    The exact middle of the case height. The panel slot and the case hole are both centred here. Its distance from the handle centre is the Gap, which is different for every lock, so enter it yourself.

Lock slots (cut in the door edge)

Example drawings, they follow the numbers on the right. The top one looks straight at the door edge (shape of the slots), the bottom one is the side of the lock (thin faceplate, box-shaped case). The numbers are what you key into the machine, allowance already added.

Machine input = actual lock size + allowance. Three separate parts: actual size, allowance, machine input. The slots are always centred on the lock-body centre, so both slots share the same “From TOP” and “From door face” numbers. Once the allowances are tuned, ask Claude to lock these numbers.

Hinges

Example drawing, it follows the numbers on the right. The yellow dot is the start point the machine needs: the slot corner nearest TOP, measured from TOP. The slot is cut in from the door edge, flush with one door face, not in the middle of the edge.

Hinge slot size

3 hinges: the 2nd is exactly in the middle between the 1st and 3rd. 4 hinges: the 2nd is a fixed 70 gap from the 1st, and the 3rd is exactly in the middle between the 2nd and 4th. The 1st is 150 from the frame top and the last is 150 from the frame bottom, fixed, cannot be changed.
